# Code of conduct

We welcome contributions regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or preferred operating system.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, give constructive
feedback, and assume good intent when asking for clarification. Credit others'
work and respect their privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, spam, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to this repository's Issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content,
warn participants, restrict participation, or block accounts, depending on the
severity and persistence of the behavior. Enforcement should be proportionate
and apply consistently, including to maintainers.

For harassment or private-data exposure, use GitHub's **Report abuse** control
on the relevant content or profile. Include a link and a factual description;
do not post private evidence in a public Issue. Technical security reports should
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
